BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few things you might be wondering.

Where do you work?

We serve Calgary and surrounding communities. Share your location and the type of work when you contact us so we can confirm availability.

How do I get an estimate?

Send your project type, photos, approximate measurements and preferred timing. We will review the details and arrange a visit when the scope needs an on-site assessment.

Can you handle smaller projects?

Yes. Alongside larger renovations, we take on focused work such as doors, flooring, railings, fences and painting.

What happens if the scope changes?

Changes should be discussed and agreed before additional work proceeds, including any effect on price or timing.

Have a project in mind?

Call, text or send us the details to start the conversation.
