Privacy Policy

Updated October 10, 2026

About this policy

NOVA Home Construction uses personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare estimates and manage requested work. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, contact the Manager at info@novahomeconstruction.ca or +1 (825) 288-4475.

Information you provide

An enquiry may include your name, email, phone number, project location, description, budget, preferred dates and photos. Only share information relevant to the work. Do not upload identification documents, payment-card details, passwords or photos of people without their permission.

Purpose and consent

We use enquiry details to assess your project, contact you and prepare an estimate. Submitting a request does not subscribe you to marketing. You may choose to contact us by phone instead. To withdraw consent for optional uses, contact the Manager; some information may still be needed for requested services or legal obligations.

Hosting and service providers

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses. Google Fonts supplies the website fonts. When online submissions are enabled, Cloudflare provides the security check and request processing, and Resend sends enquiry emails to NOVA. These providers may process information in the United States and other countries, where different laws may apply. Contact the Manager at info@novahomeconstruction.ca for our current provider locations and handling practices before submitting sensitive information.

Browser storage and external links

The site stores your English/French preference in your browser. This preference does not contain your enquiry details. The website code does not include advertising trackers or analytics. A security check may process device information when the form is enabled. Facebook and Instagram have their own privacy practices when you follow those links.

Safeguards and retention

Enquiry photos are sent as email attachments, not published in our gallery. Email and service-provider systems retain information under their applicable settings and policies. We aim to limit access and retain customer information only as reasonably needed for the project and business or legal purposes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Contact us to ask about retention, access, correction or disposal of your information.

Questions and concerns

Contact the Manager at info@novahomeconstruction.ca to discuss a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before providing personal information. If your concern is unresolved, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta at oipc.ab.ca.
