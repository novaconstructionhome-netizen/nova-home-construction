BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few things you might be wondering.

Can you rebuild just the shower?

Yes. We can review a shower-only scope, including removal, preparation, waterproofing and finish work.

Can I choose my own fixtures and tile?

Yes. Confirm dimensions, quantities and product suitability with us before purchasing so the installation can be planned around them.
